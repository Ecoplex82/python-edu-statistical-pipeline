## 🚀 Key Modules & Highlights

### 1. Data Governance & Ethics Framework
- **Compliance & Governance Audit:** Implementation of purpose limitation, consent protocols, and secure data destruction schedules.
- **Privacy Debt Analysis:** 10-year risk assessment on public education datasets, identifying risks associated with unmitigated demographic and performance data retention.
- **De-Identification Protocol:** Anonymization techniques replacing student/institutional identifiers with pseudo-anonymized primary keys (`institution_id`).

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis & Statistical Modeling
- Analysis of primary, secondary, and post-secondary educational metrics across US states and counties.
- Evaluation of key variables:
  - Math & Reading Proficiency Percentages
  - Graduation Rates & Chronic Absenteeism
  - SAT / ACT Average Scores
  - AP / IB Course Participation & Broadband Access
  - Title I Status & School Locale classification
